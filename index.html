How GhostView handles privacy

Plain answers, no overpromising.

What GhostView does

  • No GhostView account and no social media login.
  • No GhostView server: nothing you search is sent to us. Recent searches stay on this device only.
  • Never likes, follows, comments, messages or interacts with anyone. GhostView sends no notifications to profile owners.
  • Only shows what Instagram and TikTok publish through their official public embeds. Private accounts stay private, and GhostView never gets around blocks or sign-in walls.

What GhostView can't promise

  • Content loads straight from Instagram and TikTok, so those platforms can see your IP address and browser, the same as visiting them logged out.
  • If you're signed in to Instagram or TikTok in this browser, their embeds may be able to read your cookies. Strict mode (below) blocks that in Chrome and Edge. For the most privacy, use a private window.
  • GhostView can't control what the platforms log on their side.
  • Downloads only happen through TikTok's own Save video option, which only appears if the creator allows it. GhostView never downloads videos itself.
Strict modeLoad embeds without your cookies (works in Chrome and Edge; other browsers ignore it). Turn off if embeds won't load.
